Our Service

We handle everything from Japan to Guyana. You just place your order and wait — we take care of the rest. We accept orders from just one vehicle.

ROUTE1

Direct Purchase from Auto Auctions

Over 3.5 million vehicles are listed annually at Japan's largest auto auctions. We purchase directly with zero intermediary fees.

ROUTE2

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Vehicles are inspected by an officially designated inspection agency approved by the Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA).

ROUTE3

Export Procedures in Japan

We handle all complex export procedures using advanced digital systems, reducing both workload and costs.

ROUTE4

Vehicle Storage & Inland Transport

We store your vehicle securely and transport it to the port safely and efficiently.

ROUTE5

Shipping

We provide full support from booking to shipment. Shipping documents such as B/L will be provided after the vessel departure.

ROUTE6

Arrival in Guyana

After your vehicle arrives in Guyana, you will complete the necessary customs procedures. We can introduce trusted local partners to support you.
